name: Waffles
---

## Ingredients

 - 3 1/2 cups flour
 - 4 tbsp sugar
 - 2 tbsp baking powder
 - 1/2 tsp salt
 - 4 eggs
 - 3 1/2 cup milk
 - 1 cup oil
 - 2 tsp vanilla

## Directions

 0. Start heating up waffle iron.
 1. Mix dry ingredients together
 2. Mix wet ingredients together
 3. Make a well in the bowl of dry ingredients and pour in the wet ingredients.
 4. Mix ingredients until wet. (You don't want to over-mix&hellip;it's okay of it's a bit lumpy).
 5. Spray cooking spray on waffle iron, and start cooking your waffles. Each waffle should go for about 4 minutes.
