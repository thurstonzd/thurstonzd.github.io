This classic cookie is an updated version of the recipe that appeared in the 1941 edition of the Better Homes and Gardens cookbook. From [Better Homes and Gardens](https://www.bhg.com/recipe/desserts/chocolate-chip-cookies/). By Sheryl Geerts

## Ingredients

 - ½ cup shortening
 - ½ cup butter, softened
 - 1 cup packed brown sugar
 - ½ cup granulated sugar
 - 1 tsp. baking soda
 - 2 eggs
 - 1 tsp. vanilla
 - 2 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
 - 1 12-oz. package semisweet chocolate pieces (2 cups)
 - 1 1/2 cup chopped walnuts, pecans, or hazelnuts (optional)


## Directions

 1. Start by preheating the oven to 375°F. 
 2. Inside of a large mixing bowl, beat shortening and butter with an electric mixer on medium-high speed for 30 seconds. You can also use just butter, or just shortening.
 3. Add brown sugar, granulated sugar, and baking soda. Beat on medium for 2 minutes, scraping the bowl occasionally. 
 4. Beat in eggs and vanilla until they are combined. 
 5. Beat in as much of the flour as you can using the mixer. Using a wooden spoon, stir in any remaining flour. 
 6. Stir in chocolate pieces and, if desired, nuts. 
 7. Drop dough by small spoonfuls 2 inches apart onto ungreased cookie sheets. 
 8. Bake for 6 to 8 minutes or until edges are lightly browned (cookies may not appear set). Cool on cookie sheets for 2 minutes. Transfer cookies to a wire rack to cool. Makes about 48 cookies. 

## Variations

### Giant Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prepare the recipe as directed, except use a ¼-cup dry measure or scoop to drop mounds of dough about 4 inches apart onto an ungreased cookie sheet. Bake in a 375°F oven for 11 to 13 minutes or until the edges of the cookies are lightly browned. Makes about 20 cookies.

### Macadamia Nut and White Chocolate Cookies

Prepare the recipe as directed, except substitute chopped white baking bars or white chocolate baking squares for the semisweet chocolate pieces. Stir in 1 3½-oz. jar macadamia nuts, chopped, with the chopped white baking bars or squares.

### Chocolate Chip Cookie Bars

Prepare the recipe as directed, except press dough into an ungreased 15x10x1-in. baking pan. Bake in a 375°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes or until the cookies are golden. Cool in pan on a wire rack. Cut into bars. Makes 48 bars.

### Chocolate Chip Cookie Pizzas

Prepare the recipe as directed, except do not stir in chocolate pieces and nuts. Press half of the dough into an ungreased 12-in. pizza pan. Sprinkle half of the chocolate pieces and half of the nuts over the top, pressing in lightly. Repeat the process with a second ungreased 12-in. pizza pan and the remaining dough and toppings. Bake in a 375°F oven for about 15 minutes or until golden. Frost as desired. Cut each pizza into 8 wedges, then cut a circle through wedges halfway between the center and the edge of the pan. Makes 32 pieces total.
