name: Pancakes

You can also freeze pancakes&hellip;they go great in the toaster!

## Ingredients

 - 3 1/2 cups of flour
 - 4 tbsp sugar
 - 4 tsp baking powder
 - 1 tsp baking soda
 - 1/2 tsp salt
 - 2 eggs
 - 3 cups buttermilk
 - 6 tbsp oil

## Directions

 1. Turn on griddle. If you can set the temp, set to around 325&deg;F.
 2. Mix dry ingredients together
 3. Mix wet ingredients together
 4. Make a well in the bowl of dry ingredients and pour in the wet ingredients.
 5. Mix ingredients until wet. (You don't want to over-mix&hellip;it's okay of it's a bit lumpy).
 6. Pour pancakes onto griddle.
 7. When they start to bubble, it's time to flip them.
